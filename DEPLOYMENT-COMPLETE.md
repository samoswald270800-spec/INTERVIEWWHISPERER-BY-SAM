### **Step 2: Connect Repository**

1. Sign in with GitHub
2. Select: `samoswald270800/interview-whisperer-online`
3. Choose branch: `main-2.0`

### **Step 3: Add Environment Variables**

Add these in your platform's dashboard:

```env
OPENAI_API_KEY=your_key_from_openai
REDIS_URL=your_redis_url_from_upstash
SESSION_SECRET=random_string_here
ADMIN_USER=admin
ADMIN_PASS=your_secure_password
NODE_ENV=production
```

**That's it!** Click "Deploy" and your app will be live in ~3-5 minutes!
